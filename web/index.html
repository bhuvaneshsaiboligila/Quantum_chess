How to play

Split sends one piece into superposition across two reachable squares at once.

Merge recombines the two halves of a split piece back onto one square.

Measurement happens whenever a quantum square is touched — the piece collapses to one real location, decided by its odds.

A king's probability of still existing is shown above; it reaches zero when that side loses.
